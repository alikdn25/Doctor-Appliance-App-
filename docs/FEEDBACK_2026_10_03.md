This report covers the seven comments received through the user's October 3 report. Later reports
should cover new comments only. Implementation is in PR #12; CI and server acceptance are recorded

## 1. Direct parts and materials pricing

Parts markup settings and automatic price adjustments are removed. Customer price is entered
directly. Parts and materials have a separate purchase-price field and a calculated difference
between the entered prices, based on quantity. Entering a purchase price never changes the quoted
customer price.

Purchase costs belong to the person who entered them. Other technicians and office users cannot
read, overwrite or infer them from document totals, price history, exports or aggregate profit.
Customer-facing documents never include them. Conversion and revision preserve the original
cost author. Existing document costs are attributed to the document creator during migration;
legacy price-book costs without a known author remain hidden. Shared business expenses and job
expense receipts retain their existing, separate permissions.

## 2. Familiar time zones

Company setup, company settings and platform company creation offer searchable labels such as
`UTC-07:00 — Vancouver, Canada` and `UTC+05:30 — Kolkata, India`, plus UTC. Search supports city,
country and offset. Stored IANA identifiers remain unchanged so daylight-saving transitions work.
Displayed offsets are current offsets; the UI explains automatic daylight-saving changes.

## 3. Visible Menu

The working header has a labeled Menu button with a 44-pixel touch target. It opens the complete
working navigation. The current page title is separate from the Menu label. Selecting a link on
a phone closes the menu drawer.

## 4. Sign in to work

Owners and office Admins land on the working dashboard; Technicians land on My jobs. Valid intended
links still work. A verified new user creates a company once and receives Owner membership and
the first brand automatically. Normal login does not open platform administration or start a
support impersonation session. Platform administration remains a separate destination.

An existing platform administrator without a working membership selects a company once and uses
Open workspace to join as themselves, or creates their own company. Later logins use that
membership directly. Support access remains separate and no longer asks for a reason in the UI.

Remember me is selected by default. The default session lifetime is 30 days; an existing server
`.env` must explicitly set `SESSION_LIFETIME=43200` to replace a previous 120-minute setting.
Suspension, logout and employee replacement continue to revoke access where appropriate.

## 5. Technician replacement and work transfer

Owners can manage team members; office Admins can manage Technicians. Transfer jobs moves all
unfinished assigned work, including waiting-for-parts jobs whose diagnosis visit has already
finished. Open visits move to the replacement; completed visits retain the former person's name
and history. Closed jobs remain unchanged. Rescheduling a transferred job updates its current
responsibility; removing the appointment keeps the unfinished job visible for scheduling.

Replace technician reuses the login email with a new name and password. It creates a new identity,
transfers unfinished work and disables the former identity and its sessions. Completed work and
private purchase costs keep their original author. Existing passwords are never displayed.
An account shared with another company cannot be replaced by one company's administrator.
Use the ordinary transfer/deactivation flow for that case.

## 6. Members access

Owners and office Admins can open Members. Admins can manage Technicians without changing an
Owner, changing their own role or promoting someone to Owner/Admin. Technician and foreign-company
access remain restricted. Working membership is used for permissions, including when the person
also has platform-administrator privileges.

## 7. Book customer

Book customer is permanently available in the working header and first in the menu for people
allowed to create jobs. Calendar has its own explicit Book customer action. Each opens the booking
form with the first visit selected; calendar booking carries the selected date. A solo company's
single assignable person is selected automatically. Ordinary unscheduled job creation remains
available.
